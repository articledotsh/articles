# Iran Conflict Escalates as Houthis Blockade Saudi Arabia and Oil Breaches $90

**July 20, 2026** — *Dubai/London/Washington*

For the ninth night in a row, American warplanes carried out bombing runs over Iranian territory early Monday, hitting cities along the Gulf coast and pushing deeper into the northwest. The strikes were the latest in a spiral of retaliation that began after three US soldiers were killed over the weekend in separate attacks in Jordan and Iraq. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cly52xzjd3qo), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/20/us-bombs-iran-for-ninth-consecutive-night-as-hormuz-tensions-escalate)

## US Strikes and American Casualties

The American military said it went after command centers, coastal defenses, missile launchers, and communication networks used by Iran to target commercial ships moving through the Strait of Hormuz. Iranian state media reported explosions in Tabriz, Chabahar, Konarak, Bandar Mahshahr, and Bandar Imam Khomeini. The attack on Tabriz stood out — it was the first time since the war began in late February that the northwestern city had been hit. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cly52xzjd3qo), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/20/us-bombs-iran-for-ninth-consecutive-night-as-hormuz-tensions-escalate)

President Trump said the bombing was meant to honor the US troops killed in recent days. Two died on Friday when a missile struck their base in Jordan, and a third was killed Saturday in northern Iraq during a routine disposal of unexploded Iranian drone munitions. The Pentagon identified the two soldiers killed in Jordan as Private Isabella Gonzales, 19, and Lieutenant Tyler James Feehan, 25. Search teams have found unidentified remains believed to belong to a fourth service member still listed as missing. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cly52xzjd3qo)

Trump posted on social media that Iran would pay "many times over" for each American killed. Since the conflict opened on February 28, 17 US service members have been confirmed dead. [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-hormuz-strait-war-july-19-2026-63996576847424ab5f22887f38037ce8)

Iran's Revolutionary Guards answered the overnight strikes by launching missiles and drones at an airport in Jordan, two military bases in Kuwait, and positions in Syria. Air raid sirens sounded across Bahrain, and Kuwait's military said it shot down several Iranian drones. The Kuwaiti government also reported that a desalination plant was hit for the second day in a row, causing a fire and disrupting fresh water production. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/7/20/iran-war-live-us-military-carries-out-another-wave-of-strikes-on-iran), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/)

## Houthi Blockade Threatens Global Oil Supply

The Houthi militia in Yemen, which has been backed by Iran for years, declared a maritime blockade against Saudi Arabia on Monday. Their military spokesman said all Saudi vessels were banned from the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, the narrow passage connecting the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden, calling it retaliation for a Saudi blockade of Yemeni ports that has lasted nearly twelve years. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cm2gmddx1ldo)

Closing Bab el-Mandeb would be disastrous for oil markets because Saudi Arabia has been routing most of its crude through the Red Sea ever since the Strait of Hormuz became too dangerous to navigate. Shipments from the Saudi port of Yanbu have risen to roughly four million barrels a day, four times the volume from a year ago, according to data from Kpler and Signal Ocean. A full closure would remove about 7 percent of global oil supply on top of the 10 percent already lost due to the Hormuz disruption. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/houthis-impose-saudi-naval-blockade-opening-new-front-us-iran-war-2026-07-20/)

Mohammed Albasha, an analyst at the Basha Report risk advisory firm, told Reuters that even if the Houthis never attack a single vessel, the mere announcement creates enough uncertainty to drive up shipping insurance costs and divert trade away from Saudi ports. With Hormuz already effectively shut, a simultaneous closure of Bab el-Mandeb would trap roughly a quarter of the world's daily oil and gas supply behind two simultaneous chokepoint closures. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/20/yemens-houthis-declare-naval-blockade-of-saudi-arabia-what-to-know)

## Oil Markets and Diplomacy

Brent crude jumped 2.5 percent on Monday and briefly touched $90.32 a barrel before settling back as traders digested signals that diplomacy might not be completely dead. Iran's foreign ministry confirmed it had received ceasefire proposals from unnamed mediators, which helped calm some of the panic buying. Still, US gasoline prices hit $4 a gallon, and crude has climbed about 20 percent in July alone. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/20/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-crude-us-iran-centcom-hormuz.html), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/07/20/brent-hits-90-as-widening-middle-east-conflict-rattles-oil-markets/)

A senior Iranian official told Reuters that mediators had floated the idea of a ten-day ceasefire to salvage the interim agreement signed in mid-June, which was supposed to reopen the strait and freeze the fighting. That deal has been unraveling since Trump declared it dead on July 8. Iran's interior minister also traveled to Pakistan for the second time in a week, asking Islamabad to resume its role as a go-between. [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/middle-east/yemen-houthis-maritime-embargo-saudi-arabia-oil-rcna588345)

Secretary of State Marco Rubio said the US remained open to a diplomatic resolution but insisted that any deal had to be substantive, not just a temporary pause. He urged other nations to contribute hardware or funding to keep commercial shipping moving through the Gulf. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/20/us-bombs-iran-for-ninth-consecutive-night-as-hormuz-tensions-escalate)

Goldman Sachs told clients that its scenario of Brent hitting $100 a barrel was now realistic. The combination of two simultaneous blockade threats, civilian infrastructure being hit on both sides, and the failure of June's interim deal means markets are pricing in prolonged disruption rather than a quick resolution. Hundreds of thousands of people in Iran, Lebanon, and now the Gulf states are feeling the effects — through blackouts, water shortages, and steadily more expensive fuel. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/07/20/brent-hits-90-as-widening-middle-east-conflict-rattles-oil-markets/)
