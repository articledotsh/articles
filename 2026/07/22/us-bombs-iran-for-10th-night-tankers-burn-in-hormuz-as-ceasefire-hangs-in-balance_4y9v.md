**July 21, 2026** — *Dubai/Washington/Tehran*

American warplanes struck Iranian territory for the tenth night running early Tuesday, hitting Qeshm Island — the largest island in the Strait of Hormuz — along with the port cities of Bandar Abbas and Bushehr. Iran's Revolutionary Guard said it set two oil tankers ablaze in the strait and launched missiles at US military assets in Bahrain and Kuwait. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/21/hormuz-tankers-on-fire-as-us-iran-continue-attacks-whats-the-latest), [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-hormuz-strait-war-july-19-2026-63996576847424ab5f22887f38037ce8)

## Tenth Night of Strikes

US Central Command said it hit Iranian military command centers, coastal defense systems, missile and drone launch sites, and air defense networks. Explosions were reported across at least six Iranian provinces including Fars, Hormozgan, Ilam, Kerman, and Sistan-Baluchistan. The strikes on Shiraz — a city better known for its ancient poetry and gardens — came around 1 a.m. local time and marked a notable expansion of the bombing campaign beyond Iran's southern coast. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/21/us-launches-tenth-consecutive-night-of-attacks-on-iran)

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed it destroyed a US Patriot air defense system in Bahrain's Al-Riffa area and hit radar installations in Muharraq. In Kuwait, the IRGC said it struck a long-range radar site, a telecommunications hub, satellite receivers, and an MQ-9 drone hangar at Ali Al Salem airbase. The Kuwaiti government confirmed multiple facilities were hit but said air defenses intercepted most incoming projectiles. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/7/21/iran-war-live-us-launches-10th-night-of-strikes-tehran-attacks-kuwait), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/21/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-houthis.html)

## Tankers on Fire in the Strait

The IRGC announced that two oil tankers attempting to cross the Strait of Hormuz through what it called an unsafe southern route suffered explosions and broke into massive fires. Rescue teams were sent to evacuate the crews. The UK Maritime Trade Operations agency separately reported that a vessel was hit off the coast of Oman, forcing its crew to abandon ship. According to Lloyd's List Intelligence, only three commercial ships managed to transit the strait on Sunday — a sliver of the roughly twenty million barrels of oil and gas that once passed through daily before the war. [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/iran-strait-of-hormuz-tehran-united-arab-emirates-dubai-b3018674.html), [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-hormuz-strait-war-july-19-2026-63996576847424ab5f22887f38037ce8)

President Trump posted on Truth Social that Iran would pay many times over for every American killed. The Pentagon confirmed 17 US service members have died since the conflict began on February 28, and nearly 100 have been injured since the US restarted strikes on July 7. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said the war has so far cost $37.5 billion, with the Pentagon preparing to request billions more. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news), [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/iran-strait-of-hormuz-tehran-united-arab-emirates-dubai-b3018674.html)

## Houthi Blockade and Oil Markets

Yemen's Houthi rebels followed through on their threat to blockade Saudi Arabia, announcing a maritime embargo on all Saudi vessels passing through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. The Saudi-led coalition responded by saying it would use force to keep the waterway open. The standoff puts roughly 2.5 million barrels per day of Saudi oil exports at risk, according to Rystad Energy, at a time when the Strait of Hormuz is already effectively closed. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/21/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-houthis.html)

Brent crude traded at $90.20 a barrel on Tuesday, up 1.1 percent, after swinging wildly on news of the blockade and the ceasefire talks. US gasoline prices hit $4 a gallon, adding political pressure on the White House ahead of the midterm elections. Rystad's head of geopolitical analysis warned that if no ceasefire emerges and the Houthi threat intensifies, the risk of a significant oil price rebound would be substantial. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/21/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-houthis.html)

## Ceasefire or Escalation

Mediators from Qatar, Egypt, Pakistan, and Turkey have presented both sides with a proposal for a ten-day ceasefire that would fully reopen both shipping lanes in the Strait of Hormuz. Under the plan, Iran would stop attacking vessels in exchange for the US lifting its naval blockade, and negotiators would use the pause to clarify the ambiguous language in last month's collapsed interim agreement that both sides blame for the current fighting. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/21/iran-war-ceasefire-proposal-trump-troops), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/07/21/mediators-seek-to-salvage-us-iran-ceasefire-with-10-day-truce-proposal/)

Trump has not accepted the proposal. US officials say the president is focused on retaliating for the deaths of American soldiers and has not ruled out a much larger joint campaign with Israel to force Tehran's capitulation. Israeli Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich, however, said Israel has no interest in joining the renewed US military campaign against Iran. The White House told Al Jazeera that strikes will continue until Trump decides otherwise, but added that diplomatic talks remain ongoing. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/21/hormuz-tankers-on-fire-as-us-iran-continue-attacks-whats-the-latest), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/21/iran-war-ceasefire-proposal-trump-troops)
